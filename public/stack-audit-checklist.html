5-Minute Stack Audit Checklist

Score your lead capture and follow-up system in 7 questions.

Answer each question honestly. Check the box if the statement is true for your business today. At the end, count your checks to see where you stand. Most businesses score 2-3 out of 7 on their first pass.

Lead Response

1. Every inbound lead gets a response within 5 minutes.

After-hours, weekends, and holidays included. If a lead fills out a form at 9 PM Friday, someone (or something) reaches out before they close the browser tab.

2. Missed calls trigger an automatic follow-up.

When you miss a call, the lead automatically gets a text, email, or voicemail drop within 2 minutes. No manual effort required.

CRM & Tracking

3. Every lead is tracked in a CRM with stage progression.

Not a spreadsheet. Not sticky notes. A real CRM where you can see every contact, every touchpoint, and every deal stage at a glance.

4. You know your lead-to-customer conversion rate.

If someone asked "what percentage of your leads become paying customers?" you could answer immediately with a real number, not a guess.

Follow-Up System

5. Cold leads get a multi-touch re-engagement sequence.

Leads that didn't convert on first contact get an automated 14-day sequence (email, SMS, call) without anyone on your team remembering to follow up manually.

6. You have at least 10 Google reviews with a 4.5+ star rating.

An active review campaign asks every satisfied customer for a review automatically. Your online reputation is growing without manual outreach.

Ownership

7. You own all your tools, data, and workflows.

If you fired your agency or tech vendor tomorrow, would everything keep running? You hold the credentials, the code, and the data. No lock-in.

Score Yourself

6-7 checks Your stack is solid. You're ahead of 90% of SMBs.
4-5 checks Good foundation. A few automations could double your efficiency.
2-3 checks You're leaving money on the table. This is where most businesses are.
0-1 checks Every lead is a coin flip. The good news: small changes make a big impact here.
