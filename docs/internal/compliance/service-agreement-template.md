> **This is a working draft. It is not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by counsel licensed in Texas or any other jurisdiction. Do not send this document to a client until a Texas-licensed business attorney has reviewed and redlined it.**
>
> **What to ask your attorney to verify:**
>
> 1. The limitation-of-liability cap is appropriate for an AI-services freelance practice in Texas (typical cap = 12 months of fees paid or $50K, whichever is greater).
> 2. The indemnification provisions are balanced and Texas-enforceable (Texas has specific rules around indemnification clauses for negligence).
> 3. The IP-ownership language correctly distinguishes client-owned Deliverables from ClearAutomations' Background IP and methodology (this is the section freelancers most often get wrong).
> 4. The vendor-pass-through clause is enforceable (i.e., the client agreement reflects the operational reality of pass-through billing without creating joint-and-several liability).
> 5. The dispute resolution clause aligns with your preferences (mediation-then-arbitration vs Texas state court).
> 6. The independent contractor language complies with Texas + federal worker-classification rules (1099 / non-employee).
> 7. The data-handling and confidentiality language is consistent with the BAA template (when the BAA is signed alongside, it controls; when no BAA is signed, this section controls).
> 8. Force majeure, assignment, and governing-law clauses match standard Texas business-services practice.
>
> **Budget for attorney review:** $1,000–$2,500 for a redline pass on this document + the BAA template. Allow 1–2 weeks turnaround.

# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of *[Effective Date]* (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**ClearAutomations LLC**, a Texas limited liability company with its principal place of business at *[Address]* ("**ClearAutomations**" or "**Service Provider**"), and

*[Client Legal Name]*, a *[entity type]* with its principal place of business at *[Address]* ("**Client**").

ClearAutomations and Client are each referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, ClearAutomations provides AI-powered front-desk automation, workflow design, voice-agent configuration, and related professional services to small and mid-sized businesses;

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage ClearAutomations to provide certain Services as set forth in one or more Statements of Work executed pursuant to this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Background IP**" means any intellectual property, including software code, workflow templates, configuration patterns, written methodology, and know-how, owned or developed by ClearAutomations prior to or independently of the Services performed under this Agreement.

1.2 "**Deliverables**" means the work product specifically created for Client and identified as such in a Statement of Work, including without limitation custom configuration files, client-specific written copy (e.g., website copy, email templates), and client-specific workflow customizations.

1.3 "**Services**" means the professional services to be performed by ClearAutomations as described in a Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a document substantially in the form of Exhibit A, executed by both Parties, that describes the specific Services, Deliverables, fees, and timeline for an engagement.

1.5 "**Vendor Pass-Through Costs**" means amounts payable directly by Client to third-party vendors (including but not limited to hosting providers, voice-agent platforms, telephony providers, CRM platforms, email providers, and AI / LLM API providers) for tools and services that are part of, but distinct from, the Services. Vendor Pass-Through Costs are not included in ClearAutomations' fees.

1.6 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Engagement via SOWs.** This Agreement is a master agreement. The specific Services to be performed will be described in one or more SOWs executed by both Parties. Each SOW, upon execution, is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement.

2.2 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states that it overrides a specific provision of this Agreement.

2.3 **Productized SKU Framework.** ClearAutomations offers productized SKUs structured in phases (Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3), each described in the applicable industry vertical's published materials. The SOW will specify which phase(s) and which add-ons are within scope.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Fees.** Client will pay ClearAutomations the fees set forth in the applicable SOW (the "**Fees**"). Fees are stated in U.S. dollars and exclude all applicable sales, use, value-added, and similar taxes (other than ClearAutomations' income taxes).

3.2 **Pilot Engagements.** Where the SOW specifies a "pilot" or "case-study" engagement, the Fees may be $0 or otherwise reduced, in exchange for the case-study terms specified in the SOW.

3.3 **Vendor Pass-Through Costs Are Separate.** Vendor Pass-Through Costs are billed directly to Client by the applicable vendor and are not invoiced by ClearAutomations. ClearAutomations is not responsible for any vendor pricing, vendor performance, or vendor billing disputes. ClearAutomations will, upon Client's request, assist with vendor selection and configuration, but Client retains ownership of all vendor accounts opened in Client's name during the engagement.

3.4 **Invoicing.** ClearAutomations will invoice Client for setup fees upon SOW execution (50% on execution, 50% on Deliverables completion, unless the SOW specifies otherwise) and for monthly retainer fees on the first business day of each month, payable Net-15.

3.5 **Late Payments.** Invoices not paid within 30 days of the invoice date will accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. ClearAutomations reserves the right to suspend Services if any invoice is more than 30 days past due, after providing written notice and a 10-day cure period.

3.6 **Refunds.** Fees paid are non-refundable except where ClearAutomations materially fails to perform and fails to cure within the cure period in Section 10.3.

## 4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated as provided in this Section 4.

4.2 **SOW Term.** Each SOW will have its own term as specified therein. Termination of a SOW does not terminate this Agreement; this Agreement remains in effect for so long as any SOW is in force.

4.3 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party. Client remains responsible for fees accrued through the termination effective date.

4.4 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days after receipt of written notice describing the breach.

4.5 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination of this Agreement or any SOW:
   (a) ClearAutomations will deliver to Client all completed Deliverables and any in-progress work product reasonably capable of delivery;
   (b) Client retains ownership of all vendor accounts opened in Client's name;
   (c) Client will pay ClearAutomations all accrued and unpaid fees through the termination effective date;
   (d) The provisions of Sections 5 (Intellectual Property), 6 (Confidentiality), 9 (Limitation of Liability), 10 (Indemnification), and 11 (Insurance) will survive termination.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 **Deliverables.** Upon Client's payment in full of all fees due under the applicable SOW, ClearAutomations assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to the Deliverables specifically created for Client and identified as such in the SOW. Client owns the Deliverables and may use, modify, and distribute them without further consent or payment to ClearAutomations.

5.2 **Background IP.** ClearAutomations retains all right, title, and interest in and to ClearAutomations' Background IP, including without limitation:
   (a) Generic workflow templates, configuration patterns, and design methodology;
   (b) Written processes, checklists, and operational doctrine;
   (c) Software code, scripts, and tools developed prior to or independently of this engagement;
   (d) Aggregated, anonymized performance data and benchmarks.

5.3 **License to Background IP.** ClearAutomations grants Client a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use ClearAutomations' Background IP solely to the extent incorporated into the Deliverables and as necessary for Client's internal business use of the Deliverables.

5.4 **Client Materials.** Client retains all right, title, and interest in and to any materials, content, data, branding, or information provided by Client to ClearAutomations ("**Client Materials**"). Client grants ClearAutomations a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Client Materials solely for the purpose of performing the Services.

5.5 **Aggregate / Benchmark Data.** ClearAutomations may collect, analyze, and use anonymized and aggregated performance data derived from the Services (e.g., average response times, no-show rate baselines, conversion patterns) for the purpose of improving the Services, publishing industry benchmarks, and developing case studies. ClearAutomations will not disclose Client-identifying information without Client's prior written consent.

## 6. CASE STUDY AND PUBLICITY

6.1 **Case Study Use.** Where a SOW specifies a pilot or case-study engagement, Client grants ClearAutomations the right to:
   (a) Identify Client as a ClearAutomations client by name and logo in marketing materials and on ClearAutomations' website;
   (b) Publish anonymized performance metrics derived from the engagement (e.g., "X% improvement in lead response time") provided that Client has reviewed and approved the specific metrics and language in writing before publication;
   (c) Record a brief client testimonial (audio, video, or written) upon mutual agreement.

6.2 **Approval Right.** Client retains the right to review and approve any case-study materials before they are published. ClearAutomations will not publish Client-identifying materials over Client's reasonable objection.

6.3 **Reversal.** Client may revoke case-study consent upon ninety (90) days' written notice, after which ClearAutomations will remove the Client-identifying materials from active marketing channels (though historical use need not be retracted).

## 7. WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMERS

7.1 **ClearAutomations Warranties.** ClearAutomations represents and warrants that:
   (a) It will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards;
   (b) It has the right to enter into and perform under this Agreement;
   (c) The Deliverables, as delivered, will not knowingly infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

7.2 **No Other Warranties.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN SECTION 7.1, CLEARAUTOMATIONS PROVIDES THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES "AS IS" AND DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT. CLEARAUTOMATIONS DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SERVICES OR DELIVERABLES WILL ACHIEVE ANY PARTICULAR BUSINESS OUTCOME, CONVERSION RATE, REVENUE INCREASE, OR OTHER RESULT.

7.3 **AI-Generated Output Limitations.** Client acknowledges that the Services may incorporate output from third-party artificial intelligence systems (including large language models). Client acknowledges that AI-generated output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or biased; that AI systems may behave unpredictably; and that Client is responsible for reviewing all AI-generated output before relying on or publishing it.

7.4 **Client Warranties.** Client represents and warrants that:
   (a) It has the right and authority to enter into this Agreement;
   (b) It will provide accurate and complete information to ClearAutomations as needed for the Services;
   (c) Client Materials do not infringe the rights of any third party;
   (d) Client will comply with all applicable laws and regulations in its use of the Services and Deliverables, including without limitation laws governing telemarketing (TCPA), data privacy (HIPAA where applicable, Texas HB 300 where applicable), and advertising of professional services.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential under the circumstances, including without limitation business plans, financial information, client lists, technical specifications, and pricing.

8.2 **Obligations.** The Receiving Party will:
   (a) Use Confidential Information solely to perform under this Agreement;
   (b) Protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care;
   (c) Disclose Confidential Information only to its employees, contractors, and advisors with a need to know who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

8.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:
   (a) Was rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure;
   (b) Becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party;
   (c) Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations;
   (d) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

8.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is compelled by law to disclose Confidential Information, it will provide prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party (where legally permitted) so the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order.

8.5 **PHI Distinction.** Where this Agreement is accompanied by an executed Business Associate Agreement, the BAA governs the treatment of Protected Health Information; this Section 8 governs non-PHI Confidential Information. Where no BAA is in force, Client agrees not to disclose Protected Health Information to ClearAutomations and to limit Confidential Information disclosed to ClearAutomations to non-PHI business information.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 **Cap.** EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM (i) A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 10, (ii) BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY UNDER SECTION 8, (iii) CLEARAUTOMATIONS' INDEMNIFICATION OF IP INFRINGEMENT, OR (iv) GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF (A) FIFTY THOUSAND U.S. DOLLARS ($50,000) OR (B) THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO CLEARAUTOMATIONS UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

9.2 **No Indirect Damages.** IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOST DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT, REGARDLESS OF THE LEGAL THEORY OR WHETHER THE PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **ClearAutomations Indemnity.** ClearAutomations will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client from any third-party claim that the Deliverables, as delivered by ClearAutomations and used as intended, infringe such third party's U.S. patent, copyright, or trade secret, subject to the limitations in Section 9.

10.2 **Client Indemnity.** Client will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless ClearAutomations from any third-party claim arising from:
   (a) Client Materials infringing third-party rights;
   (b) Client's use of the Services or Deliverables in violation of applicable law (including TCPA, HIPAA, state professional advertising rules);
   (c) Client's modification of the Deliverables after delivery;
   (d) Disclosure of Protected Health Information to ClearAutomations outside the scope of an executed BAA.

10.3 **Indemnification Procedure.** The Party seeking indemnification will (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party in writing of the claim; (b) grant the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement; (c) reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. INSURANCE

11.1 **ClearAutomations Insurance.** ClearAutomations will maintain, at its own expense and during the term of this Agreement:
   (a) Errors and Omissions (Professional Liability) insurance with coverage of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 aggregate;
   (b) Cyber Liability insurance with coverage of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 aggregate.

11.2 **Certificate of Insurance.** Upon Client's request, ClearAutomations will provide a Certificate of Insurance evidencing the coverages required in this Section 11.

## 12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

12.1 **Relationship.** ClearAutomations is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency, or franchise between the Parties. Neither Party has authority to bind the other or create any obligation on behalf of the other.

12.2 **Tax Responsibility.** ClearAutomations is solely responsible for its own taxes, including self-employment taxes. ClearAutomations is not entitled to employee benefits from Client, including without limitation healthcare, retirement, paid time off, or workers' compensation.

## 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

13.2 **Dispute Resolution.** The Parties will attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through informal negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the Parties will submit the dispute to non-binding mediation in *[County, Texas]* before resorting to litigation or arbitration. Any unresolved dispute will be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by *[JAMS / AAA — attorney to select]* in *[County, Texas]* under its commercial arbitration rules. The prevailing Party is entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

13.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delays or failures in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including without limitation acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, government action, internet or utility outages, or third-party vendor failures, provided the affected Party promptly notifies the other and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

13.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld), except that either Party may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets, upon written notice to the other Party.

13.5 **Notices.** All notices must be in writing and delivered by email (with confirmation of receipt) or by overnight courier to the addresses set forth in the preamble (or to such other address as a Party designates in writing).

13.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with any SOWs and the BAA (where executed), constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on the subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous oral or written agreements.

13.7 **Amendment.** No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.8 **Waiver.** No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party granting the waiver. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

13.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the unenforceable provision will be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

13.10 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original. Electronic signatures (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, or similar) have the same legal force as wet signatures.
