# Demo Client Profile: Lone Star Roof & Restoration

## Fictional Business

**Lone Star Roof & Restoration** is a fictional storm restoration contractor serving the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

- Service area: Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Arlington, Denton
- Team: 2 owners, 1 office manager, 4 sales reps, 6 crews
- Current tools: website form, shared inbox, phone provider, HubSpot pipeline, CompanyCam-style photo storage
- Main services: hail damage inspections, wind damage inspections, leak response, insurance restoration, roof replacement, gutter add-ons

## Business Problem

The company wins when it responds quickly and documents clearly, but storm weeks create operational pileups.

- Missed calls spike after hail.
- Inspection scheduling happens across calls and texts.
- Photo and claim documents arrive in scattered channels.
- Adjuster appointment and claim follow-up depend on memory.
- Estimates and contracts stall without a clear follow-up cadence.

## Demo Positioning

Clear Automations shows how a contractor-owned workflow can:

1. Capture storm damage leads.
2. Score urgency and route the lead.
3. Book or request an inspection callback.
4. Collect photo/document status.
5. Track claim and adjuster follow-up.
6. Follow up on estimates and unsigned contracts.
7. Ask for reviews after completed work.
8. Send a weekly storm pipeline digest.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real homeowners, payment details, access instructions, private customer notes, claim documents, adjuster files, or real contractor credentials.
