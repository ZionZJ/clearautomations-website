# Client Demo Script

Use this script to show a roofing prospect the demo without implying real client results or promising claim outcomes.

## Opening

"This is a fictional Texas roofing demo. It uses fake data only. The point is to show how storm leads, missed calls, inspections, photos, insurance follow-up, estimates, contracts, reviews, and owner reporting can move through one contractor-owned system."

## Step 1: Storm Damage Intake

Show `01 - Storm Damage Intake`.

Talking points:

- The form captures homeowner lead context.
- Hail, wind, leak, storm date, and photo readiness help prioritize follow-up.
- The workflow returns a clear inspection or callback next action.

## Step 2: Missed Storm Call Callback

Show `02 - Missed Storm Call Callback`.

Talking points:

- A missed call after hail should become a task immediately.
- The workflow prioritizes recent calls, damage language, repeat callers, and in-service-area leads.
- Speed-to-inspection is the core revenue lever.

## Step 3: Inspection Scheduler

Show `03 - Roof Inspection Scheduler`.

Talking points:

- Qualified leads get routed into inspection slots or dispatcher callback tasks.
- The system does not promise an inspection time unless the contractor approves the rule.

## Step 4: Photo and Document Collection

Show `04 - Photo Document Collection`.

Talking points:

- The workflow sends a safe checklist instead of letting photos scatter across texts.
- It can request claim number status without asking for full claim documents in public forms.

## Step 5: Insurance Claim Follow-Up

Show `05 - Insurance Claim Follow-Up`.

Talking points:

- The workflow tracks claim stage and stalled steps.
- It does not promise coverage or legal advice.

## Step 6: Estimate and Contract Follow-Up

Show `06 - Estimate and Contract Follow-Up`.

Talking points:

- Open estimates and unsigned agreements get an owner-approved follow-up cadence.
- Lost reasons become visible.

## Step 7: Post-Job Review Request

Show `07 - Post-Job Review Request`.

Talking points:

- Happy completed jobs get review requests.
- Unresolved jobs route to manager follow-up first.

## Step 8: Weekly Storm Pipeline Digest

Show `08 - Weekly Storm Pipeline Digest`.

Talking points:

- The owner sees storm leads, inspections booked, claim follow-up, stalled estimates, reviews, and workflow failures.
- This is the operational proof that the system is catching leaks.

## Close

"The first paid step is a roofing workflow audit. We map where your storm leads and claim follow-up leak today, then build the smallest automation that recovers revenue without replacing your reps, adjuster communication, or roofing judgment."
