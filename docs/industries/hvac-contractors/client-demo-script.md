# Client Demo Script

Use this script to show an HVAC prospect the demo without implying real client results.

## Opening

"This is a fictional HVAC contractor demo. It uses fake data only. The point is to show how missed calls, emergency requests, stale estimates, reviews, and maintenance renewals can move through one contractor-owned automation system."

## Step 1: Emergency Service Intake

Show `01 - Emergency Service Intake`.

Talking points:

- The public form captures business-safe service context.
- No-cool calls are scored above tune-ups and general estimates.
- The workflow returns a clear next action for callback or dispatcher handoff.

## Step 2: Missed Call Callback Queue

Show `02 - Missed Call Callback Queue`.

Talking points:

- A missed call is not treated as lost.
- The workflow prioritizes emergency phrases, time of day, and repeat attempts.
- The owner can see what needs action first.
- In production, the caller also gets a missed-call text-back within seconds of an unanswered call ("Got your call, we'll be right with you, reply here"). That message only fires after A2P 10DLC registration is complete and includes opt-out language.

## Step 3: Estimate Follow-Up

Show `03 - Estimate Follow-Up`.

Talking points:

- Replacement quotes often need structured follow-up.
- The workflow classifies quote age and next message.
- Lost reasons become visible instead of staying anecdotal.

## Step 4: Review Request

Show `04 - Post-Job Review Request`.

Talking points:

- Completed jobs are filtered before asking for reviews.
- The system avoids asking unhappy customers for public reviews.
- Review requests become consistent without making the technician remember.

## Step 5: Maintenance Reactivation

Show `05 - Maintenance Plan Reactivation`.

Talking points:

- Old tune-up and maintenance lists are low-risk revenue opportunities.
- The workflow segments expired, active, and high-value households.
- Seasonal campaigns can run before the next heat wave.

## Step 6: Weekly Owner Digest

Show `06 - Weekly Owner Digest`.

Talking points:

- The owner gets a simple summary of leads, booked jobs, stale estimates, review opportunities, and failed workflow items.
- This is where the automation proves what it recovered.
- The weekly digest rolls up into a 90-day ROI report: recovered calls, booked-job value, and response-speed trend.

## Close

"The first paid step is a $1,000 AI Assessment. We map where your calls and estimates leak today and quantify what recovery is worth. The fee credits toward your build if you move forward within 60 days."
