# Demo Client Profile: Lone Star Comfort Co.

## Fictional Business

**Lone Star Comfort Co.** is a fictional HVAC contractor serving the Dallas-Fort Worth suburbs.

- Service area: Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, Richardson, Garland, Carrollton
- Team: 2 owners, 1 dispatcher, 8 technicians, 2 installers
- Current tools: website form, shared inbox, phone provider, HubSpot starter pipeline, spreadsheet maintenance list
- Main services: emergency AC repair, seasonal tune-ups, replacement estimates, maintenance plans

## Business Problem

The company gets enough demand, but response speed breaks down during heat spikes.

- After-hours no-cool calls hit voicemail.
- Estimate requests get one manual follow-up, then stall.
- Maintenance plan renewals are inconsistent.
- Reviews depend on whoever remembers to ask.
- The owner cannot quickly see where leads were lost.

## Demo Positioning

ClearAutomations shows how a contractor-owned workflow can:

1. Capture emergency and estimate requests.
2. Score urgency and route the lead.
3. Create or update the CRM record.
4. Trigger the right callback or follow-up.
5. Ask for reviews after completed jobs.
6. Send a weekly owner digest.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real customers, payment details, access instructions, private customer notes, technician notes, or real dispatch credentials.
