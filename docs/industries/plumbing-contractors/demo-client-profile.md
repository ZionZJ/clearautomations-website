# Demo Client Profile: Bayou City Pipe & Drain

## Fictional Business

**Bayou City Pipe & Drain** is a fictional plumbing contractor serving the greater Houston area.

- Service area: Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, Spring, Cypress, The Woodlands
- Team: 2 owners (one is the responsible master plumber), 1 dispatcher, 6 licensed plumbers, 2 apprentices
- Texas state license: TSBPE M-99999 (fictional demo number, not a real license; TX requires the license number on advertising)
- Current tools: website form, Housecall Pro, shared inbox, phone provider, HubSpot starter pipeline
- Service lines: emergency repair, water heaters (tank and tankless), whole-home repipes, sewer and drain, fixture install and repair

## Realistic Fake Volumes

- About 220 inbound calls per month; roughly 30 hit voicemail after hours or on weekends.
- About 45 estimates sent per month across repipe, water heater, sewer, and fixture work.
- Average ticket: $450 service call, $2,300 tank water heater, $6,400 tankless, $9,800 repipe, $12,500 sewer line.

## Why Speed Matters in Plumbing

- A missed call is worth $285 to $1,200 in lost revenue depending on the job.
- Emergency plumbing calls close at 12 to 16 percent, the highest close rate of the trades, because the emergency caller books whoever answers first.
- 85 percent of callers who reach voicemail never call back.

## Business Problem

The company gets enough demand, but response speed breaks down nights and weekends.

- Burst-pipe and no-water calls hit voicemail at 2am Saturday, when the job is biggest.
- Gas-smell and sewage calls need a human instantly, and the current process has no guaranteed path for that.
- Repipe and water heater estimates get one manual follow-up, then stall.
- Reviews depend on whoever remembers to ask.
- The owner cannot quickly see where leads were lost.

## Demo Positioning

ClearAutomations shows how a contractor-owned workflow can:

1. Capture emergency and estimate requests around the clock.
2. Score urgency on the 5-tier plumbing taxonomy and route the lead.
3. Send safety emergencies (gas smell, sewage backup, major flooding) to a human immediately, never an AI queue.
4. Create or update the HubSpot record.
5. Trigger the right callback or follow-up sequence.
6. Ask for reviews after completed jobs.
7. Send a weekly owner digest.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real customers, payment details, access instructions, private customer notes, technician notes, or real dispatch credentials.
