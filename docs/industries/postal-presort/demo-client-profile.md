# Demo Client Profile: Lone Star Mail Services

## Fictional Business

**Lone Star Mail Services** is a fictional Texas presort vendor serving the Austin-San Antonio corridor.

- Service area: Austin, San Antonio, San Marcos, New Braunfels, Round Rock
- Team: 1 owner, 1 office manager, 4 mailroom operators, 1 driver
- Current production stack: Window Book DAT-MAIL, PostalOne!, NCOA processing, in-house intelligent inserter
- Current lead funnel: contact form, shared inbox, no CRM, occasional outbound by owner
- Main services: First-Class presort, Marketing Mail commingling, address cleaning (CASS / NCOA), IMb tracking
- Specialty: handles handwritten addresses (rare moat in the industry)

## Business Problem

The company has 26 years of operations experience and strong local relationships, but the inbound funnel has dried up.

- New inquiries come in evenings and weekends; voicemail catches them.
- Past inquiries (30+ days old) sit in the office manager's email; nobody works the list.
- Cold outbound to mailroom directors is the obvious growth lever, but TCPA + state advertising rules + DNC scrubbing block the team from running it cleanly.
- Postage rates have risen 12-15% cumulative since early 2025; mailers are looking for solutions, but the company isn't surfacing in their searches.

## Demo Positioning

Clear Automations shows how a presort-company-owned funnel can:

1. Capture inbound form submissions and route to a 30-45 second AI callback.
2. Score and 5-way branch each call (qualified / no-answer / not-interested / email-only / DNC).
3. Run a daily Tier 1 (past inquiries 30+ days) + Tier 2 (proximity leads) outbound dial queue with SMS warm-up.
4. Re-engage ghosted deals with a 14-day multi-channel sequence.
5. Run attorney-cleared cold outbound to Apollo-sourced mailroom-director lists with DNC scrub and B2B-exemption documentation.
6. Send daily and weekly pipeline digests to the owner.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real client contact details, real client mail-piece content, end-recipient PII, payment details, or real production-stack credentials.
