# Demo Client Profile: Cedar Bend Dental Studio

## Fictional Business

**Cedar Bend Dental Studio** is a fictional Texas general dentistry practice.

- Service area: North Austin and Cedar Park
- Team: 2 dentists, 3 hygienists, 1 office manager, 4 front desk and treatment coordinators
- Current tools: website form, shared inbox, PMS, reminder tool, spreadsheet treatment-plan follow-up list
- Main services: general dentistry, hygiene, crowns, implants consults, whitening, emergency exams

## Business Problem

The practice has demand, but the front desk workflow leaks production.

- Missed calls are not always called back fast enough.
- No-shows and late cancellations create open chair time.
- Hygiene recall outreach is inconsistent.
- Treatment plans stall after the consult.
- The owner cannot quickly see where follow-up is stuck.

## Demo Positioning

Clear Automations shows how a practice-owned workflow can:

1. Capture business/practice audit leads without PHI.
2. Prioritize missed new-patient calls.
3. Classify fake no-show and cancellation records.
4. Segment fake recall records.
5. Surface fake treatment plans needing follow-up.
6. Match schedule gaps with eligible fake contacts.
7. Filter completed fake visits for reviews.
8. Send a weekly practice digest.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real patient names, clinical details, diagnosis details, treatment details, insurance IDs, appointment reasons, x-rays, payment card data, or real practice credentials.
