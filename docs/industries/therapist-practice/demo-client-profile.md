# Demo Client Profile: Cedar Park Counseling Collective

**Status:** Fictional demo client. Use fake data only.

## Practice Snapshot

- Name: Cedar Park Counseling Collective
- Location: Cedar Park, Texas
- Practice type: Therapy group practice
- Providers: 8 clinicians, 1 intake coordinator, 1 billing/admin lead
- Monthly sessions: about 260
- Public channels: website form, phone calls, Psychology Today, Zencare, Headway
- Current tools: SimplePractice, Google Workspace, Twilio demo number, Google Sheets demo dashboard

## Demo Business Problems

- Prospective clients submit inquiry forms after hours and wait until the next business day.
- Consult reminders are inconsistent because the intake coordinator works from email and calendar memory.
- Directory listings drift out of date, especially specialties, availability, and accepted payer language.
- Leadership wants to understand Phase 2/3 opportunities without using live PHI in a demo.

## Demo Promise

The demo shows a safer first step:

1. Capture practice/business lead data only.
2. Reject or flag submissions that appear to include patient-specific data.
3. Route accepted inquiries into a callback queue.
4. Send generic consult reminders.
5. Maintain a monthly directory health check.
6. Show fake-data simulations for intake handoff and session-to-claim reconciliation.

## Data Boundary

Allowed in the demo:

- Practice name
- Business contact name
- Work email
- Work phone
- Website
- Provider count
- Current EHR/tools
- Broad admin bottleneck
- Monthly session volume

Not allowed:

- Patient names
- Symptoms
- Diagnoses
- Insurance member IDs
- Appointment reasons
- Clinical notes
- Therapy progress notes
- Psychotherapy notes

## Sales Demo Positioning

Use Cedar Park Counseling Collective to show a client what a polished implementation could look like without implying ClearAutomations already has a live therapist case study. Keep the language as a demo model, not a client result.
