# Demo Client Profile: Lone Star Drain & Pipe Co. (default; swap per sub-vertical)

## Default Fictional Business: Plumbing

**Lone Star Drain & Pipe Co.** is a fictional Texas plumber serving the DFW area.

- Service area: Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Arlington
- Team: 2 owners, 1 dispatcher, 4 plumbers, 1 apprentice
- Current tools: website form, Jobber CRM, shared inbox, phone provider, paper estimate pad
- Main services: emergency drain clearing, water heater replacement, fixture install, slab leak repair, leak detection
- Texas state license: TSBPE M-12345 (TX requires license number on advertising)

## Business Problem

The company gets enough demand, but response speed breaks down during work hours and after.

- Emergency calls (active flooding, gas smell, water heater failure) hit voicemail during job-site work.
- Estimate requests get one manual reply, then disappear into texts and inboxes.
- Reviews depend on whoever remembers to ask.
- The owner can't quickly see where leads were lost.

## Demo Positioning

ClearAutomations shows how a trades-owned workflow can:

1. Capture inbound emergency and estimate requests.
2. Score urgency by sub-vertical (plumbing: leak / clog / no-water / fixture / water-heater).
3. Trigger sub-vertical safety branches when needed (active flooding → gas-smell → 911 if applicable).
4. Create or update the CRM record.
5. Trigger callback or estimate-followup sequence.
6. Ask for reviews after completed jobs.
7. Send a weekly owner digest.
