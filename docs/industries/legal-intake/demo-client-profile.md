# Demo Client Profile: Lone Star Personal Injury Group

## Fictional Business

**Lone Star Personal Injury Group** is a fictional Texas solo personal-injury practice serving the Austin metro.

- Service area: Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, San Marcos
- Team: 1 attorney (lead), 1 paralegal, 1 part-time intake coordinator
- Practice areas: motor vehicle accidents (MVA), premises liability, work injury
- Current case management: Clio
- Current intake: contact form, phone (rolls to attorney's cell after hours), answering service after hours
- Annual revenue: $1.5M, ~50 contingency cases per year

## Business Problem

The firm wins when it responds first to inquiries, but the inbound funnel leaks 30-40% of after-hours leads to faster-responding firms.

- Answering service takes a name + number; doesn't run a conflict check; doesn't qualify the matter type.
- After-hours inquiries wait until 9 AM the next business day for a callback — by which time the prospect has called 2-3 other firms.
- The paralegal spends 3-4 hours per week declining tire-kicker inquiries (jurisdiction issues, SOL slippage, conflicts) that could have been declined at intake.
- 1-2 engagement letters per quarter ghost after being sent — the firm doesn't know if the prospect signed with another attorney, lost interest, or just got busy.

## Demo Positioning

Clear Automations shows how a firm-owned funnel can:

1. Capture inbound form submissions and route to a conflict-cleared callback inside 4 business hours.
2. Run a conflict-check gate via the Clio API before any fact-gathering.
3. If clear, collect minimum-info, schedule a 15-min consult call with the intake coordinator, and route to the right practice area.
4. If conflict, decline politely and end the call.
5. Run a 3-touch engagement-letter follow-up sequence with DocuSign integration.
6. Track statute of limitations weekly with 90 / 60 / 30 / 14-day warnings.
7. Send a weekly firm digest with conversion-by-practice-area metrics.

## Demo Boundaries

Use fake data only. Do not use real prospective clients, real opposing parties, real fact patterns, real privileged information, real case files, or real Clio credentials.

## Sales Demo Positioning

Use Lone Star Personal Injury Group to show prospective firm clients what a polished implementation could look like. ClearAutomations does not (yet) have a published legal-intake case study; keep the language as a demo model, not a client result.

## Compliance Posture for Demo

Even in fictional demo mode, the demo conversations and call recordings must:

- Open with the AI disclosure + recording disclosure
- Stay within the conflict-gate architecture (no fact-pattern collection before the gate clears)
- Avoid any legal advice or case-merit discussion
- Reference Texas state bar rules where applicable

This makes the demo itself a compliance teaching moment, not just a feature demo.
